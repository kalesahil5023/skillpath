Privacy policy

Clear about what currently stays on your device.

This policy describes the current SkillPath website as implemented. It will be updated if the product’s data practices change.

Information currently stored

SkillPath’s My Plan feature uses your browser’s localStorage under the key skillpath_my_plan. It stores the plan you choose, including your recommended path and skill, your selected time commitment and goal, your recommendation reason, seven checklist tasks, and their completion state.

This information stays in the browser on the device where you save it. SkillPath does not currently send it to a server or require an account.

Information SkillPath does not currently collect

The current website does not provide email collection, user accounts, payment processing, a contact form, analytics tracking, or affiliate tracking. SkillPath does not claim to collect information through those features while they are not implemented.

Third-party resources

Resource links may take you to third-party websites. Those services have their own privacy practices, cookies, and terms. Review their policies before using them.

Future changes

If analytics, email, affiliate tracking, or other data-related features are added later, SkillPath should update this policy and implement any notices or choices that are appropriate to the feature and location.

Your choices

You can clear your saved My Plan through the Reset Plan button, or by clearing SkillPath site data in your browser settings. Clearing site data may remove other data stored for this website.
