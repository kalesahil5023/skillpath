Editorial standards

How SkillPath chooses and presents resources.

Resources are meant to support practical learning and informed decisions, not to create artificial urgency or promote a tool simply because it pays a commission.

Evaluation criteria

Usefulness

Does the resource help with a clear beginner task, skill, or next step?

Legitimacy

Is it an official source, established provider, or otherwise credible option?

Beginner suitability

Is the learning curve, documentation, and expected use understandable for a newcomer?

Pricing and value

Are the free and paid options described plainly enough for an informed choice?

Availability

Could access, pricing, location, platform support, or eligibility limit who can use it?

Limitations

Are important trade-offs, costs, policies, or reasons it may not fit made clear?

Review and transparency

When a resource is reviewed or updated, SkillPath records its date checked and should re-check time-sensitive details such as pricing, access, and program terms before making a recommendation. Availability and provider policies can change, so visitors should confirm current details directly with the provider.

Affiliate relationships

An affiliate label appears only when SkillPath has configured both an active, verified relationship and a valid affiliate URL. Otherwise the official non-affiliate link is used. Affiliate status is considered alongside usefulness, legitimacy, value, suitability, availability, and limitations—not in place of them.

Alternatives

Where a paid resource is shown, SkillPath considers whether a free, lower-cost, or non-affiliate alternative is useful to mention. The goal is to help a visitor choose, not to pressure them toward a purchase.

Corrections

Resource information can become outdated. A verified contact method will be published before public correction requests are invited. Until then, visitors should rely on the resource provider’s own current information.
