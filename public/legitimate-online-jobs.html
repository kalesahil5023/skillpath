Online jobs guide

Look for legitimate online jobs by matching evidence of skill to a clear role.

A remote job search takes time. Focus on roles with transparent responsibilities, real employers, and requirements you can honestly meet.

Start with realistic beginner roles

Potential entry points include customer support, virtual assistance, spreadsheet and administrative support, content assistance, junior design support, and social media assistance. Read the full description and choose roles that match your current skills rather than applying to everything labelled remote.

Prepare useful evidence

Use a clear, honest resume and a profile that names the kind of work you seek. Add a few relevant samples: a cleaned spreadsheet, design support sample, writing piece, portfolio page, or content calendar. Tailor the application to the role and save a copy of what you submitted.

Research the employer and the process

Prefer official company career pages and established job platforms. Check that the company website, role, email domain, and interview steps make sense together. Legitimate employers should be able to explain the work, hiring process, and pay arrangement clearly.

Online earning scams to avoid

Never pay a recruiter or employer to secure a job. Do not deposit a cheque, buy equipment from an unknown sender, or share passwords and codes. Treat vague work, unusually high pay, rushed pressure, and requests for sensitive information as warning signs. Verify independently before acting.

Plan your next step

Use Path Finder to see whether online jobs are a suitable starting direction. Build a relevant sample through a beginner skill roadmap, then use My Plan to track a week of focused preparation.
